# ADR 0001: Record architecture decisions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-09

## Context

SiteSense is built solo with AI coding tools and published openly. Reviewers and future
contributors need to know why things are the way they are, not only what they are.

## Decision

Record significant decisions as short ADRs in `docs/adr/`, numbered and never rewritten. A
changed decision gets a new ADR that supersedes the old one.

## Consequences

A few minutes per decision, and a clear audit trail of design reasoning.
